A character design exploration sheet for an original mascot character.
A clean 3 by 2 grid of six clearly different full-body design directions for Mascot, a warm, capable little maker who keeps an eye on the work, all on the same seamless warm off-white (#F6F3EF) background, equal scale, each figure fully visible with generous spacing, no dividers, no labels, no text.

Shared brief: a compact creature with an oversized, softly squared head blending into a small pear-shaped body, short legs and small rounded mitten hands. Signature silhouette idea: two short rounded tabs set diagonally at the upper corners of the head (not bunny ears, not fox ears) and two sturdy splayed feet, which together make a subtle four-way X shape without being a literal letter. Face: an inset oval face plate of soft matte charcoal fabric (not a screen, not a visor), warm cream oval eyes with dark round pupils and tiny natural catchlights, a small understated friendly curved mouth sewn as a raised cord, and two small muted coral cheek appliques. Palette family: coral red body, deep warm charcoal face fabric, warm cream eyes. Personality: warm, observant, quietly competent.

Vary between the six: the head-to-body ratio, the exact shape of the silhouette feature, the shape of the face area and the size and spacing of the eyes, the stance, and the amount of visible seam detail. Keep the same material and the same palette family in all six so that the choice is about design, not rendering style.

Fabric: very fine short micro-suede needle-felt plush, sculptural rather than shaggy. Dense, short, fine fibres with clearly visible individual filaments that curl slightly and vary in density and direction; a soft matte surface with no gloss, no plastic, vinyl or silicone sheen and no wet highlights; a few pale flyaway fibres catch the light along the silhouette; the fibres are thin (about one pixel wide at full resolution) and crisp, never blurry blobs or smooth airbrushed gradients; slightly uneven needle-felted surface, hand-made but premium; seams are hand-sewn, gently wandering rather than ruler-straight; soft self-occlusion in creases and under the head. Colour: coral red (#F05C63) body fabric with warmer, lighter tones on lit fibre tips and deeper tones in the pile and crevices; deep warm charcoal (#24252B) for the face fabric, matte, never a screen or glass.

Lighting: a large soft window key from the upper left, a gentle fill from the right, a subtle warm rim on the silhouette and a soft bounce from the floor; neutral exposure and white balance; soft shadows with gentle self-occlusion. Physically plausible, photographed-looking, like a premium collectible or product photograph of a real handmade object, not a painted illustration and not a glowing fantasy render.

Hard exclusions: no tail, no nose, no teeth, no claws, no antennas, no clothing, no fingers, no mechanical joints. No text, lettering, logos, symbols or watermark anywhere. Nothing glossy, rubbery, wet or translucent; no CGI-smooth skin; no long hair-like fur; no extra limbs; no costume or accessory that is not listed. Original character. It must not resemble any existing mascot, toy or brand character; in particular it is not a beige hooded mascot.
